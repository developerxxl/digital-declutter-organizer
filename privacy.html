Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Digital Declutter Organizer ("the app") analyzes your content on your device. There is no account, no advertising, no third-party analytics and no tracking.

Data that stays on your device

  • Photos and videos. With your permission, the app reads your photo library to find duplicates, screenshots, large videos, similar and blurry photos. Images, thumbnails and analysis results never leave your device.
  • Contacts. With your permission, the app reads contacts to find duplicates and incomplete cards. Before any change, a vCard backup is saved on your device. Contact data never leaves your device.
  • Calendars. With your permission, the app reads calendars and past events so you can remove old events. Event data never leaves your device.
  • Files. The app reads only folders you pick, on your device.
  • Your settings, the Review Box, the history of what you removed, and the optional first name you enter.

Data that leaves your device

  • Purchases. Payments are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether your Pro access is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipts. It does not receive your name, email, photos or contacts.
  • Referral program. Only if you open the referral program, the app stores a random device identifier, your referral code, how many people used your code, and whether you earned a free month, in our database hosted by Supabase. Codes you enter are checked against this database.

What we never do

We never upload your photos, videos, contacts, calendars or files. We never sell your data, show ads, or share your data with data brokers.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings and tap "Erase app data". Deleting the app removes all local data. To remove your referral record, contact us from the contact page.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
