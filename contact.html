I deleted a photo by mistake

Open the Photos app → Albums → Recently Deleted. Items stay there for 30 days. In the app, Settings → History lists what you removed and when.

Why can't the app clear System Data?

iOS manages System Data and caches itself and does not let any app clear them. The app's Storage Guides show Apple's own steps.

How do I restore Pro?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone or iPad, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Mac, open the App Store → your account → Subscriptions.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings in the app and tap "Erase app data". See our Privacy Policy.
