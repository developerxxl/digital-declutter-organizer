Digital Declutter Organizer

Clear space on your iPhone, iPad and Mac by reviewing duplicate photos, screenshots, large videos, similar shots, duplicate contacts and old calendar events. You decide what goes. Nothing leaves your device.

Every item waits in a Review Box first, and Photos keeps deleted items in Recently Deleted for 30 days. No account, no ads, no tracking.
